October 22, 2026

Liholiho Elementary visits CIRP

Liholiho Elementary students took turns moving a physical robot arm and trying the same movements in simulation. At a second station, they watched a humanoid robot dance and answer questions.

Huaijin Chen, Riki MacMillan and Christian Moore hosted the robotics activities during the students’ visit to UH Mānoa’s Department of Information and Computer Sciences.

Hawaiʻi FFA teacher training

Lukas Moroz, Wilson Huynh, and Kanta Saito demonstrated robotic arms for K–12 educators at a Hawaiʻi FFA training event.

September 2026

Coconut Rhinoceros Beetle Symposium

Christian Moore, Riki MacMillan and Huaijin Chen presented the lab’s palm robotics project at the third annual Coconut Rhinoceros Beetle Science and Management Symposium at UH Mānoa.

The team showed how a physical robot arm and its simulated counterpart let researchers test movements around a palm tree and generate examples for training a robot policy.

2025 Farm Robotics Challenge

How do you count pineapples across a field and measure the size of each fruit? At Dole Plantation, Team ʻĀina used a drone to count pineapples from the air and a ground robot to build 3D views of individual fruit.

The UH Mānoa undergraduate team earned the $10,000 Excellence in Productivity (Air) award with support from a faculty advising team led by Huaijin Chen.

Plug and Play Summit

Team ʻĀina was invited to the summit after winning its 2025 Farm Robotics Challenge award. Student lead Christian Komo explained how the team uses a drone and a rover to map pineapple blooms for harvest planning. Teammates Kai Garcia and Mikhail Shkaralevich joined him with adviser Huaijin Chen.
