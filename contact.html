Prospective graduate students can apply to the UH Mānoa ICS graduate programs. The lab typically offers research assistantships only to students who have already worked here.

Please email Huaijin Chen about lab openings only if you have worked with one of his collaborators or published relevant work at a major venue such as ICCP, CVPR, ECCV, ICCV, IROS, ICRA, RSS, or SIGGRAPH.

Current UH undergraduates and master’s students can contact Huaijin Chen about research opportunities. Undergraduates can also apply through the Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP).

Applicants for teaching assistantships must meet the university’s English proficiency requirements.
