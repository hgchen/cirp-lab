2025 Farm Robotics Challenge

How do you count pineapples across a field and measure the size of each fruit? At Dole Plantation, Team ʻĀina used a drone to count pineapples from the air and a ground robot to build 3D views of individual fruit.

The UH Mānoa undergraduate team, advised by faculty led by Huaijin Chen, earned the $10,000 Excellence in Productivity (Air) award.
