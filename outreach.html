Liholiho Elementary visits CIRP

Liholiho Elementary students took turns moving a physical robot arm and trying the same movements in simulation. At a second station, they watched a humanoid robot dance and answer questions.

Huaijin Chen, Riki MacMillan and Christian Moore hosted the robotics activities during the students’ visit to UH Mānoa’s Department of Information and Computer Sciences.

Coconut Rhinoceros Beetle Symposium

Christian Moore, Riki MacMillan and Huaijin Chen presented the lab’s palm robotics project at the third annual Coconut Rhinoceros Beetle Science and Management Symposium at UH Mānoa.

The team demonstrated how they use a robot arm and its simulated counterpart to test movements around a palm tree and generate examples to train a robot control policy.
