Jun 12, 2025

CVPR 2025 PIRA Workshop and Bin Picking Challenge

Agastya Kalra was a lead organizer of the Perception for Industrial Robotics Automation (PIRA) workshop at CVPR 2025. Huaijin Chen and Yang Qian served on the organizing team. The workshop included the results and awards session for the Perception Challenge for Bin-Picking. OpenCV organized the challenge with Intrinsic, BOP, and the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

For the challenge, Yang developed, documented, and tested CIRP Lab’s reference implementation for object detection and six-degree-of-freedom (6DoF) pose estimation. OpenCV’s submission guide links to the implementation and pretrained models.

Polarization-Based Visual Computing
