Plug and Play Summit

Team ʻĀina was invited to the summit after winning its 2025 Farm Robotics Challenge award. Student lead Christian Komo explained how the team uses a drone and a rover to map pineapple blooms for harvest planning. Teammates Kai Garcia and Mikhail Shkaralevich joined him with adviser Huaijin Chen.
