Prospective students

Prospective graduate students can apply through the UH Mānoa ICS graduate programs. Research assistantships are typically offered to students who have already worked in the lab.

For inquiries about openings, please email only if you have worked with one of the PI’s collaborators or published relevant work in a major venue such as ICCP, CVPR, ECCV, IROS, ICRA, RSS or SIGGRAPH. The lab is unable to respond individually to all inquiries.

Current UH undergraduate and master’s students can contact the PI about research opportunities. Undergraduates can also apply through UROP.

International applicants interested in teaching assistantships should review the university’s English proficiency requirements.
