CVPR 2025 PIRA Workshop and Bin Picking Challenge

Agastya Kalra co-led the Perception for Industrial Robotics Automation (PIRA) Workshop at CVPR 2025. Huaijin Chen and Yang Qian served on the organizing team. The workshop presented the results of the Perception Challenge for Bin-Picking, which OpenCV organized with Intrinsic, BOP, and UH Mānoa.

Yang developed, documented, and tested CIRP Lab’s baseline for detecting industrial parts and estimating their 6DoF poses. OpenCV’s submission guide links to the lab’s code and pretrained models as a reference for participants.
